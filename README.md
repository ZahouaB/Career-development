# Project title
Final project for the Building AI course

## Summary
An AI-powered career development platform that helps individuals discover fulfilling career paths by analyzing their skills, education, experience, interests, and aspirations. The platform identifies potential career opportunities, highlights skill gaps, and recommends personalized learning pathways to support career growth and successful transitions.

## Background
After many years in the same type of role, it is natural to consider a career change. Most professionals want their work to remain meaningful, engaging, and aligned with their interests, strengths, and long-term aspirations. When this is no longer the case, career development becomes an important way to explore new opportunities and prepare for future roles.

One challenge is identifying career paths that are a good match for an individual's experience, skills, strengths, interests, and ambitions. Another is understanding which additional competencies may be required and how best to develop them.

I am currently facing this challenge myself. I would like to gain a clearer understanding of which career paths are well suited to my profile, what skills and experience they require, and how I can develop them through a structured plan that combines targeted learning with relevant practical experience.

## How it is used
The solution would be used by individuals who are considering a career change or seeking greater meaning, engagement, and fulfillment in their professional lives. 

Several groups of stakeholders would be affected by such a solution:
•	Individuals seeking a career transition, who would benefit from more informed career decisions and personalized development plans.
•	Employers, who could benefit from a workforce that is better aligned with employees' interests, strengths, and career goals. Employees who actively choose their roles are likely to be more motivated, engaged, and productive.
•	Human Resources departments and recruitment agencies, which could use the solution as a coaching and career development tool to support employees and candidates in exploring future career opportunities.
•	Universities, training providers, and certification bodies, which could gain valuable insights into emerging skill needs and use this information to develop new courses and learning programs.
•	Governments and public institutions, which could use aggregated insights to support workforce planning, identify future skill shortages, and guide investments in education and reskilling initiatives.
•	Healthcare and social services, which may indirectly benefit as better career alignment contributes to improved well-being, job satisfaction, and reduced stress, burnout, or mental health challenges among workers.

## Data and AI techniques
The project would require access to data from a large number of individuals. Relevant data should include educational background, certifications, skills, roles held, key responsibilities, career aspirations, and feedback on how well different positions aligned with personal and professional goals.

This data could be sourced from professional networking platforms such as LinkedIn, as well as from a dedicated platform where individuals voluntarily provide and maintain their own career information.
By analyzing patterns across a large number of career journeys, the system could identify roles that are a strong match for an individual's profile, highlight skill gaps, and recommend personalized learning and development opportunities to support career growth and transitions.

Given the complexity of career decisions and the many factors that influence professional success and satisfaction, advanced AI techniques such as deep learning and neural networks could help uncover relationships and career patterns that are difficult to identify through traditional analysis. These insights could be used to provide personalized career recommendations and development pathways tailored to each individual's background, interests, and goals.

## Challenges
Data accuracy is critical to the effectiveness of the solution. Robust validation and verification mechanisms would be needed to ensure that the information used by the system is reliable and up to date. This could include checks of educational qualifications, certifications, employment history, and other professional credentials through trusted third-party verification providers, similar to the background verification services offered by companies such as Semac in Norway. Ensuring high-quality data would help improve the accuracy and credibility of the career recommendations generated by the platform.

## What next
I believe that if such a solution proves successful, it should be expanded beyond experienced professionals and adapted for students and young adults who are making educational and career choices for the first time.

By analyzing a student's interests, strengths, personality traits, and aspirations, the system could suggest potential career paths and the educational programs that are most likely to lead to those careers. This could help individuals make more informed decisions at an early stage and reduce the risk of pursuing studies that do not align with their long-term interests and goals.

Over time, the solution could evolve into a comprehensive career guidance platform that supports individuals throughout their entire professional journey. It could help users explore career options, identify skill gaps, recommend learning opportunities, track their development, and provide guidance at different stages of life, from education and early career decisions to career changes and continuous reskilling.

The platform could also provide valuable insights to employers, educational institutions, and policymakers by identifying emerging skill demands, workforce trends, and future training needs. This could contribute to a better alignment between education, workforce development, and labor market needs.

## Acknowledgment 
The inspiration for this project comes from a challenge that I have observed repeatedly throughout my professional life. Many people experience a misalignment between their education, their work environment, their skills, and the type of work that truly motivates them. This challenge is becoming increasingly important as people work longer careers and as rapid technological developments continuously change the skills required in the labor market.
Career paths are no longer as linear or predictable as they once were. Individuals often need to reskill or upskill multiple times during their working lives, yet many struggle to understand which career opportunities are available to them, what skills they need to develop, and how to make informed career decisions.
This project is inspired by the idea that AI could help individuals navigate these challenges by providing personalized career guidance, identifying potential career paths, highlighting skill gaps, and recommending relevant learning opportunities. My goal is to explore how AI can help people find work that is both meaningful and aligned with their abilities, interests, and long-term aspirations.
